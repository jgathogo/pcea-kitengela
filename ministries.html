Programmes

Ministries

Groups meet in the congregations. Parish-level leaders coordinate the same work across all six.

Sunday worship

Worship is in the congregation, not only at Township. Holy Communion, baptism and confirmation follow PCEA practice under the Session and the parish minister.

Woman’s Guild

The Guild prays, visits and organises practical help. It is commissioned in some congregations (Township, Bethsaida and Neema in recent parish papers) and elects parish-level officials from those local arms.

Presbyterian Church Men’s Fellowship

PCMF gathers the men of the parish for prayer, visitation and the work of the congregations.

Youth Fellowship

Young people meet in the congregations. Parish youth work is coordinated across the six churches.

Church School

Children are taught the faith on Sunday in the congregations that run a church school. Confirmation classes sit under Christian Education.

Boys and Girls Brigade

Brigade is active in some congregations (Township, Bethsaida and Stoni Athi in recent parish papers). Parade and training dates are set locally.

Christian Education

The board watches teaching for children, youth and adults, including confirmation.

Evangelism

Outreach and witness across Kitengela, reporting to Session through the parish group.

Health Board

Health teaching and medical camps. The parish has hosted a women’s health and wellbeing gathering for Kitengela and neighbouring towns.

Board for Social Responsibility

Practical care and social witness, as Session directs.

Justice, Peace, Reconciliation and Creation

JPRC is the parish board for peace, justice and care of creation.

How to take part

Start in your congregation. To join a fellowship, enrol a child, or talk about membership, use the membership page or email info@pceakitengela.org.
