Privacy

How this website handles information.

Who we are

This site is operated by PCEA Kitengela Parish, Presbyterian Church of East Africa. The parish office is at Kitengela Township Church, Nairobi-Namanga Road, Kitengela. Contact: info@pceakitengela.org.

What this site collects

The public pages are static. We do not run a membership database or advertising trackers on this website. The membership form opens your own email application; the message goes to the parish office and is not stored on the web server.

If you email the parish, we keep that correspondence for pastoral and administrative purposes, as any church office would.

Embedded services

The homepage embeds a Google Map of Township Church. Google may set its own cookies when you use the embed. Parish email on pceakitengela.org is separate from this public website.

Questions

Write to info@pceakitengela.org if you want a copy of information the parish holds about you as a member, or if you want it corrected.
