Membership

Register as a member

Membership is through the Session. You are placed in one congregation and one district.

How membership works

PCEA Kitengela Parish welcomes worshippers in all six congregations. To join the membership roll, speak with the Session. Usually you:

  • Attend worship at the congregation you hope to join, and meet that district elder.
  • Speak with the parish minister or Session Clerk about baptism, confirmation, or transfer from another PCEA congregation.
  • Complete the parish’s membership particulars so Session can place you in a congregation and a district.

You can begin that conversation after a Sunday service, at the parish office in Township Church, by phone on +254 710 287930, or by emailing info@pceakitengela.org. The form below opens that email with your details filled in.

This opens your email app to write to info@pceakitengela.org. You can also visit the parish office at Township Church on a weekday.
