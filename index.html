Our mission

One parish, six congregations

To be a great and dynamic godly model church for holistic service in pursuance of the Great Commission.

PCEA Kitengela Parish is not a single-church parish. The Session shepherds six congregations across Kitengela. Each has its own Local Church Committee. The parish office sits at the mother church, Kitengela Township.

Session

The Parish Session, with the minister as Moderator, oversees the whole parish: doctrine, membership, and shared ministry.

Congregations

Township, Stoni Athi, Bethsaida, Ebenezar, Neema and Bethel each worship locally and report to Session through their LCC.

Parish office

The accountant, secretary and parish staff work from Township Church. That is where to find the Session Clerk on a weekday.

How the parish is organised

Sunday worship

When we meet

Each congregation sets its own Sunday order. Confirm the hour with your LCC or at the parish office. Visitors are welcome at every congregation.
