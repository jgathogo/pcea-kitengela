Six congregations

Where we gather

One Session. Six Local Church Committees. Start at the congregation nearest you.

How to read this page

These six names are the congregations named in the parish’s own 2026 Session documents. Street pins for the younger churches are still being collected. For a Sunday hour or a district, ask that congregation’s LCC or call the parish office at Township.

Kitengela Township Church

The mother church and the seat of the parish office. Nairobi-Namanga Road, Kitengela (plus code GX94+3R5). The present sanctuary was dedicated on 26 October 2014.

Come here for Session business, weekday office hours (Monday to Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM), and Sunday worship. Elders organise the congregation in districts.

Stoni Athi Church

A congregation of the parish with its own LCC and elders. The sanctuary was dedicated on 12 February 2017 by Rev. Peter Kania, then Secretary General.

Bethsaida Church

A congregation of the parish, with elders and an LCC. Groups of the parish that meet in several churches, including Woman’s Guild and Brigade, are active here.

Ebenezar Church

A congregation of the parish. The parish spells the name Ebenezar in current Session papers. Ask the LCC for the meeting place and Sunday time.

Neema Church

In Milimani, Kitengela, about four kilometres from town along the old Namanga Road. The sanctuary was dedicated on 21 August 2016 by Rev. Peter Kania. The congregation marked ten years in 2026.

Neema has church school, districts, and the usual parish groups. Milimani Presbyterian School stands on the same ground. That school is a congregation project, not the parish office.

Bethel Church

Established in August 2016. A congregation of the parish, with its own LCC and elders. Confirm the site and Sunday hour with the congregation or the parish office.

Joining

Membership is through the Session, placed in a congregation and a district. Use the register page or speak to an elder after Sunday worship.
